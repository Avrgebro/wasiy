# Subscription Trial and Access Gate

Self-serve registration (the `/registro` flow) creates one `subscriptions` row per Account: the plan, the locked unit price and currency, the billable unit count, the trial window and an `access_until` date. Until now nothing read that row; a trial ended silently and the Account kept working forever. This ADR gives the row a lifecycle, exposes it to the SPA and makes the staff surface honour it.

Payment is manual in this phase (bank transfer, Yape or Plin, arranged by email with the sales inbox). The design therefore has to work without a payment provider and must be trivially extended when one arrives: a payment only ever moves `access_until` forward and flips the status to `active`.

## Decisions

- **Gate on the date, not the status.** `Subscription::isLapsed()` compares `access_until` with now, and both the API middleware and the SPA redirect read that. The `status` column (`trialing`, `active`, `expired`, cast to `SubscriptionStatus`) is the reporting view of the same fact, kept in step by the daily `subscriptions:expire-lapsed` command. A late or missing scheduler run can never extend access.
- **No grace period at launch.** Registration sets `access_until` equal to `trial_ends_at`, which is what the confirm step told the customer. A grace period is a data decision: set `access_until` later, and every rule above still holds.
- **The API carries the state in `/me`.** `AccessContextService::accountSummary` gains a `subscription` object on `accounts[]` and `active_account`: status, plan, price, billable units, currency, trial end, access end, `days_left`, `is_lapsed` and the sales `contact_email`. `days_left` and `is_lapsed` are computed server-side so every client agrees on the countdown and the lock. Accounts created by hand have no row and get `null`.
- **402 from a middleware on the staff surface only.** `EnsureSubscriptionIsActive` wraps every staff endpoint and answers `402 Payment Required` with `code: subscription_lapsed` once the active Account has lapsed. `/me`, `/me/password` and the context endpoints sit outside the gate so the SPA can still read the state and switch accounts. The resident portal is never gated: residents are not the payer, and locking them out would hurt the customer's standing with their own community. A 402 is distinct from the 403 the SPA already maps to "deactivated".
- **Active Account resolution matches the access context.** The gate reads the session's active Account; when the session has none it falls back to the user's single accessible Account, the same implicit choice `/me` makes, so a fresh session cannot slip past the gate by skipping `/me`.
- **`subscription.manage` capability, account admins only.** Added to the matrix (ADR 0036). The SPA's account-wide pages keep gating on the account admin role as they do today; the capability is what API policies will check when payment actions exist.
- **The SPA collapses to one page.** `checkSubscriptionAccess` runs in the `/admin` layout's `beforeLoad`: a lapsed Account can only reach `/admin/subscription`, which every staff role may open, since a locked-out front desk member needs to see why. A 402 caught by the API client refetches `/me` and re-runs the guards, so stale tabs converge on the same page.
- **Banner for the last week, then the lapse.** `SubscriptionBanner` sits under the topbar in the staff shell and shows when a trial has seven or fewer days left, or whenever access has lapsed. Admins get the link to the subscription page; other staff are told to ask the account administrator. The first week of a trial stays quiet.

## Consequences

Demo and hand-made Accounts are unaffected until someone creates a subscription for them. Marking an Account paid is an update of `status` and `access_until`; the manual operations on Accounts and Subscriptions will live in a Filament panel for the Wasiy team (a separate guard, never reachable by account admins), alongside payment reminders driven by `trial_ends_at`. Per-currency formatting of plan prices uses `Intl.NumberFormat` with the subscription's currency, ahead of the general currency i18n still pending elsewhere.
