# Billing: Contracted Units, Invoices and Payment Proofs

ADR 0039 gave a subscription a lifecycle and a gate but no way to be paid. This ADR settles how Wasiy bills while payment is manual (bank transfer, Yape, Plin) and lays the rails a payment provider will later run on. Three questions are decided: what a customer pays for, what an invoice is, and how a payment is proven and confirmed.

The design has to survive two realities. Payment is confirmed by a person on the Wasiy team who checks a bank statement, so every step needs a durable record and a review queue rather than an inbox. And whatever number drives the bill will be gamed if it can be lowered for a day, so the bill cannot depend on a count taken from the database at invoicing time.

## Decisions

- **Customers pay for contracted units, never for counted ones.** `subscriptions.billable_units` is the number the customer declares, first at registration and later from the subscription page. Every invoice is for that number. The system never derives the amount from the units present in the account, so deactivating units before an invoice and reactivating them after changes nothing.
- **The contracted number is a hard cap.** An account cannot hold more active units than it contracted. Creating or reactivating a unit past the cap fails with a clear message and an "Ampliar plan" action; bulk import respects the same rule. Deactivating a unit frees a slot but never lowers the bill. Counting active units is safe here because it only restricts, it never discounts.
- **Increases apply immediately, decreases at renewal.** Raising the contracted number unlocks slots at once and the difference lands on the next invoice (prorated once a provider exists). Lowering it is allowed any time but takes effect on the next period, and only if the active units fit under the new cap then. A pending decrease shows on the subscription page as "Desde el 1 de octubre: 30 unidades". The plan's included units are the floor.
- **An invoice is a row per billing period.** `invoices` carries the subscription, the period covered, the contracted units and unit price at issue time, the amount, a due date and a status: `pending`, `under_review`, `paid`, `rejected`. A daily command issues the next invoice a fixed number of days before `access_until`. Nothing is issued during the trial.
- **Confirming a payment is what extends access.** When the team marks an invoice paid, `access_until` moves to the end of the period covered and the subscription status becomes `active`. Enforcement stays on `access_until` (ADR 0039), never on invoice status: an unpaid invoice does not lock anyone before the date the customer was told.
- **Payment proofs hang off the invoice.** From the subscription page's payment history, a pending or rejected invoice offers "Confirmar pago", which opens a drawer with a dropzone for an image or PDF and optional payment date, method, amount and operation number. Uploading moves the invoice to `under_review`. Files use the photo pipeline's limits and live on a private disk behind signed URLs, since they are bank documents. An invoice may collect several proofs; the latest is the one under review.
- **Review happens in Filament.** The Wasiy team sees the proof, confirms or rejects with a reason. Confirming marks the invoice paid and extends access; rejecting returns the row to a state where the customer can upload again, with the reason visible. The staff app only reads and uploads.
- **Emails at each transition.** To the team when a proof arrives; to the account admin when an invoice is issued, when a payment is confirmed and when a proof is rejected. Reminders before `access_until` are driven by the invoice's due date.
- **Only account admins see billing.** The subscription page, its history and the upload drawer require the account admin role. Other staff never see plans, prices or payment details; on a lapse they land on the lock screen (ADR 0039).
- **Out of scope for now.** Partial payments (one invoice, one confirmed payment), invoices or tax documents issued by Wasiy, cancellation (a subscription simply lapses), and card storage. The Portafolio plan stays a conversation with sales.

## Consequences

The units API gains one rule on create, reactivate and import. The subscription payload in `/me` or a dedicated subscription endpoint exposes contracted units, active units and any pending change so the page can show "Unidades contratadas 40 · en uso 37". The Filament panel, already planned for manual account operations, becomes the payment review queue and so is on the critical path for the first invoice. Delivery order: invoices, the issuing command, Filament review and access extension first; then the staff-side history table with row states and the upload drawer; then the emails; the unit cap can ship with any of them since it only restricts.
